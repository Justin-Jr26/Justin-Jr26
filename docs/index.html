JUSTIN J. / AI TALENT INTELLIGENCE

My work centers on the talent and research ecosystem of foundation models — especially large language models, multimodal models, and image/video generative models. I follow how technical advances relate to research teams, engineering specializations, and career possibilities.

我的工作主要围绕基座模型的人才与研究生态，尤其关注大语言模型、多模态模型，以及图像和视频生成模型。我也持续研究技术进展如何对应研发团队、工程分工与职业发展机会。

Beyond the model itself, I explore Agent applications and AI infrastructure to better understand the broader engineering landscape. This site is a collection of independent observations and openly sourced notes — a growing index rather than a finished publication.

在基座模型之外，我也关注 Agent 应用和 AI 基础设施，从更完整的技术链路理解研发工作。这是我持续整理公开研究资料与独立观察的个人空间，内容会逐步积累，而非一次性完成。

POSITIONING NOTE — I work as an AI talent advisor and industry observer; I do not claim to have personally trained or developed the models described here.身份说明：我的主要身份是 AI 人才顾问与行业观察者，不会将自己表述为亲自研发或训练相关模型的算法研究员。
