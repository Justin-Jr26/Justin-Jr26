JUSTIN J. / AI TALENT INTELLIGENCE

My work spans collaborations with leading foundation-model teams across China. By staying close to both hiring needs and the people within these organizations, I maintain first-hand visibility into emerging roles, team developments and the capabilities in demand.

我与国内头部基座模型团队保持广泛而深入的合作，能够直接接触一线招聘需求；同时与多个团队的内部成员保持长期、紧密的交流，持续了解团队发展动态与人才需求变化。

Beyond job descriptions, I follow research papers, model releases and industry shifts to understand where technology and opportunity meet. My focus covers LLM training, multimodal foundations and generative models; I also track Agent applications and AI infrastructure. I share independent perspectives based on conversations and information that can be made public.

在招聘信息之外，我也长期关注研究论文、模型发布、技术路线与行业热点，把团队的一线观察与公开技术资料结合起来，形成对技术风向和职业机会的独立判断。核心专注 LLM、多模态、生成模型训练，并延伸关注 Agent 与 AI Infra；公开分享以适合披露的信息为限。

POSITIONING NOTE — I work as an AI talent advisor and industry observer; I do not claim to have personally trained or developed the models described here.身份说明：我的主要身份是 AI 人才顾问与行业观察者，不会将自己表述为亲自研发或训练相关模型的算法研究员。
